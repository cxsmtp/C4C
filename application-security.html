Application security consulting

Ship software that stands up to attackers and auditors. We find the weaknesses that matter, help you fix them, and build security into the way your team works.

What we do

Security assessments and penetration testing

Manual, expert-led testing of web apps, mobile apps and APIs, guided by the OWASP Top 10 and ASVS. Every finding comes with proof, impact and a clear fix.

Threat modelling and architecture review

Workshops with your engineers to map how data flows, where trust changes hands, and what could go wrong, before it is built.

Secure code review

Targeted review of authentication, authorisation, input handling and cryptography, combined with tuned static analysis.

DevSecOps and supply chain

Security checks inside CI/CD: dependency and secret scanning, container and infrastructure-as-code checks, signed builds and SBOMs.

Cloud and API security

Review and hardening of AWS, Azure and GCP configurations, identity and access, API gateways and network boundaries.

Compliance readiness

Practical preparation for SOC 2, ISO 27001 and PCI DSS: gap analysis, policies that match reality, and controls that produce evidence.

What you receive

Deliverables your engineers can act on and your board can understand.

  • An executive summary that explains risk in business terms
  • Detailed findings with severity, evidence and step-by-step fixes
  • A prioritised remediation roadmap with effort estimates
  • A free re-test of fixed issues within the agreed window
  • A readout session with your engineering and leadership teams

How it works

  1. Assess

    A short, focused discovery: we read the code, the pipelines and the paperwork, and talk to the people who run them.

  2. Chart

    A prioritised plan in plain language, with effort, risk and cost for each step, so you can decide with confidence.

  3. Build

    We work alongside your team to fix, design and deliver, in small reviewed increments.

  4. Hand over

    Runbooks, training and documentation, so your team owns the result when we step back.
